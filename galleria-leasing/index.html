01 — The Vision

A New Chapter
for an Iconic Address

For decades, The Galleria was the heartbeat of Fort Lauderdale retail — a gathering place for the community and a destination for visitors from around the world. Then, like many regional malls, it lost its way.

Under new ownership and with a bold redevelopment mandate, The Galleria is undergoing a comprehensive transformation. Our vision: a curated mix of luxury retail, experiential dining, wellness, entertainment, and lifestyle brands that reflects the sophistication of modern Fort Lauderdale.

This is not a renovation. This is a reinvention — and we are selectively inviting the world's most desirable brands to be part of it from the ground floor.

05 — Why Galleria

The Case for
Galleria Fort Lauderdale

01

No Luxury Competitor

There is no true luxury retail destination between Miami's Design District and Palm Beach's Worth Avenue. The Galleria fills that void — exclusively.

02

Captive Affluent Base

ZIP codes 33304, 33316, and 33308 rank among the highest HHI in Florida. This is not a commuter market — these residents live, shop, and dine locally.

03

Tourism Amplifier

12M+ annual visitors arrive by air and sea. The Galleria is the first upscale shopping destination they encounter — within minutes of FLL and Port Everglades.

04

Year-Round Season

Unlike northern markets, Fort Lauderdale has no off-season. Florida's climate, combined with a snowbird population, sustains premium retail 365 days a year.

05

Curated, Not Crowded

We are deliberately limiting tenant count to maintain positioning. Early tenants benefit from exclusivity agreements and co-tenancy clauses that protect brand equity.

06

New Ownership, Fresh Capital

This is a fully capitalized redevelopment — not a patch job. New ownership brings the resources, vision, and commitment to see this transformation through to completion.

07

First-Mover Advantage

Brands that commit now receive the best locations, the most favorable lease terms, and the right to shape the tenant mix around them. This window closes as we fill.

08

Ground-Up Infrastructure

Full MEP replacement, new HVAC, fiber throughout, EV charging, and premium parking — the bones are old, but everything else is new.
