Based in Fort Lauderdale and active across Florida, we develop enduring places that balance design excellence, exceptional lifestyle, lasting value, and long-term responsibility to the community and the environment.

Ben Shmul, President & CEO, sets the firm's strategic vision and oversees various aspects of the business across a diversified portfolio of residential, hospitality, and mixed-use assets. Under his leadership, InSite has built a reputation for executing complex, high-impact projects and transformative real estate developments, particularly along Florida's premier coastal markets.

Ben has played a pivotal role in scaling InSite's vertically integrated platform, which provides full lifecycle control from sourcing and underwriting through execution and operations — enabling the firm to enhance returns, mitigate risk, and maintain consistent quality across its portfolio.

Vertical Integration

Full-Spectrum Control.
Every Phase. Every Asset.

InSite's vertically integrated platform spans every phase of the real estate lifecycle — from initial opportunity identification through long-term asset operation. This full-spectrum capability enables InSite to maintain quality control, optimize returns, and respond decisively to market conditions at every stage of a project.

01
Acquisitions & Disposition

InSite maintains an active acquisitions program targeting hospitality, mixed-use, and residential assets across Florida's coastal and urban markets. Each opportunity is evaluated through a disciplined underwriting process that accounts for market dynamics, physical condition, entitlement potential, and repositioning upside. On the disposition side, InSite manages strategic exits with precision to maximize portfolio value and capital efficiency.

02
Finance

InSite structures each investment with a capital stack tailored to the project's specific risk profile, hold period, and return objectives. The firm maintains active relationships with institutional equity partners, family offices, and regional and national lenders. InSite's finance team oversees debt placement, equity management, financial modeling, reporting, and investor relations across all active investments.

03
Development Strategy

InSite leads the full development process from feasibility and site selection through entitlement, programming, and project launch. The firm applies a market-driven approach to product positioning — calibrating unit mix, density, use composition, and phasing strategies to maximize both design quality and commercial performance. InSite's development expertise spans ground-up construction, adaptive reuse, and complex mixed-use assemblages.

04
Architecture & Design

InSite provides active design leadership throughout each project's development, working closely with leading architects, interior designers, and landscape architects to ensure the built environment reflects the firm's standards for quality and lifestyle. InSite's involvement encompasses concept development, design management, material selection, and brand identity — with a focus on creating spaces that perform as well aesthetically as they do operationally.

05
Construction & Redevelopment

InSite manages construction processes across both ground-up development and comprehensive property redevelopments. The firm works with experienced general contractors while maintaining direct oversight of scheduling, budgeting, and quality assurance. InSite's redevelopment capabilities allow the firm to reposition and modernize existing assets while preserving the character and long-term value that define each property.

06
Asset Management

InSite actively manages its portfolio to protect and enhance value over the full hold period. Asset management responsibilities include performance monitoring, capital improvement planning, lease and contract oversight, and strategic positioning for disposition or refinancing. InSite works closely with operating partners and third-party managers to ensure alignment between day-to-day execution and long-term investment objectives.

07
Operations Management

InSite maintains direct oversight of hospitality and residential operations across its portfolio, whether managed in-house or through third-party operators. The firm's operational expertise spans hotel management, resort operations, mixed-use programming, and residential services — ensuring that each asset performs to brand standard and delivers the lifestyle experience that defines the InSite portfolio.

08
Historic Preservation

InSite has significant experience acquiring, restoring, and repositioning historically significant properties. The firm approaches adaptive reuse with deep respect for the architectural and cultural character of each asset, working within preservation guidelines to restore historic structures while elevating their performance and livability. InSite's historic projects have contributed meaningfully to the revitalization of urban neighborhoods and the preservation of South Florida's architectural heritage.
