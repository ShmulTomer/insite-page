The Leadership Team

Photo
Ben Shmul
Chief Executive Officer

Ben Shmul sets the firm's strategic vision and oversees various aspects of the business across a diversified portfolio of residential, hospitality, and mixed-use assets. Under his leadership, InSite has built a reputation for executing complex, high-impact projects and transformative real estate developments, particularly along Florida's premier coastal markets.

Ben has played a pivotal role in scaling InSite's vertically integrated platform, which provides full lifecycle control from sourcing and underwriting through execution and operations — enabling the firm to enhance returns, mitigate risk, and maintain consistent quality across its portfolio.
